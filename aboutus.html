    Our Journey

    2015
    FinCart was founded with a vision to revolutionize personal financial management through innovative technology and expert guidance.
    2016
    Launched our mobile banking application, reaching 50,000 users in the first month. Expanded services to include investment advisory.
    2017
    Achieved 100,000 active users milestone. Obtained financial services license and expanded to 15 countries.
    2018
    Introduced AI-powered financial analytics and personalized investment recommendations to our platform.
    2019
    Surpassed 250,000 users. Launched enterprise solutions for institutional clients and corporate financial management.
    2021
    Reached 400,000 users globally. Introduced cryptocurrency trading and blockchain-based services.
    2023
    Hit 500,000 active users. Expanded to 150 countries. Launched advanced wealth management features and sustainable investment options.
    2024
    Achieved $2.5B in assets under management. Recognized as industry leader in fintech innovation and customer satisfaction.
