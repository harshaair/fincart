# FinCart Tax Module - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 How to Access the Tax Module

1. Login to your FinCart account

## 📋 Tax Module Tools Overview

### 1️⃣ Tax Calculator
**Purpose:** Calculate your estimated tax liability

**What You Need:**
- Gross annual income
- Any deductions (optional - can be selected from database)

**Steps:**
1. Enter gross income
2. Select deductions or add custom amounts
3. Choose between New Regime or Old Regime
4. Click "Calculate Tax"
5. View detailed breakdown

**Output Includes:**
- Gross income
- Total deductions
- Taxable income
- Tax amount (slab-wise)
- Health & Education Cess
- Surcharge
- Total estimated tax
- Effective tax rate

**⚠️ Important:** This is for estimation only. Verify with a professional before filing.

---

### 2️⃣ Deductions Tracker
**Purpose:** Record and track your tax-deductible expenses

**What You Can Track:**
- Insurance premiums (Section 80C)
- Health insurance (Section 80D)
- Education loan interest (Section 80E)
- Charitable donations (Section 80G)
- And 8 more categories

**Steps:**
1. Click "Add Deduction"
2. Select category from dropdown
3. Enter description (e.g., "HDFC Life Insurance Premium")
4. Enter amount in rupees
5. Select date of deduction
6. Click "Add Deduction"

**Features:**
- View all your deductions
- See breakdown by category
- Visual chart of deduction distribution
- Delete entries if needed
- Summary statistics

**Tip:** Keep receipts and proofs for all deductions. You'll need them to support your ITR.

### 5️⃣ ITR Preparation Assistant
**Purpose:** Step-by-step guidance for Income Tax Return filing

**Step 1: Select ITR Form**
- **ITR-1 (SAHAJ):** For salaried individuals
- **ITR-2 (SUGAM):** For capital gains
- **ITR-3 (VJAY):** For business/professional income
- **ITR-4:** For presumptive income

**Step 2: Check Deadlines**
- Normal taxpayer: July 31
- With audit: October 31
- Revised return: December 31

**Step 3: Verify Your Data**
- View income from FinCart
- Review deductions
- Compare with Form 16

**Step 4: Create ITR Record**
- Save draft with FY and income details
- Add any additional notes
- Save for later reference

**Step 5: Document Checklist**
- Income proof (Form 16, Form 16A)
- Deduction proof (insurance receipts, etc.)
- Bank statements
- PAN & Aadhaar
- Investment statements

**Final Steps:**
- Verify all information
- Consult a professional CA
- File on income-tax.gov.in
- Keep reference number
- Maintain all documents for 7 years

**⚠️ Critical:** Always file on the official Income Tax portal. This tool is for preparation only.

## 🔢 Tax Slabs for FY 2024-25

### New Regime (Recommended for most)
| Income Range | Tax Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹3,00,000 | 0% |
| ₹3,00,001 - ₹6,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹6,00,001 - ₹9,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹9,00,001 - ₹12,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹12,00,001 - ₹15,00,000 | 20% |
| ₹15,00,001 - ₹18,00,000 | 25% |
| Above ₹18,00,000 | 30% |

**Add:** 4% Health & Education Cess on tax
**Add:** Surcharge (15% if income >₹2M, 25% if >₹5M)

### Old Regime
| Income Range | Tax Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹2,50,000 | 0% |
| ₹2,50,001 - ₹5,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹5,00,001 - ₹10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 30% |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I file my ITR using this tool?**
A: No. You must file on income-tax.gov.in. This tool helps you prepare.

**Q: Are these calculations always correct?**
A: No. Tax laws are complex and individual situations vary. Always verify with a professional.

**Q: What if I miss the filing deadline?**
A: File a revised return immediately and consult a CA about penalties.

**Q: How long should I keep documents?**
A: Keep all tax documents for at least 7 years.

**Q: Is the new regime always better than old regime?**
A: Usually, but it depends on your deductions. Compare both in the calculator.

**Q: What if my income changes mid-year?**
A: Record the actual income and consult a professional for proper classification.

**Q: Can I file ITR multiple times?**
A: Yes, you can file revised returns. Original return by July 31, revised anytime in same FY.

**Q: Do I need all Form 16 copies?**
A: Yes, keep original for your records and upload copy if required.

**Q: What if my employer hasn't given Form 16?**
A: Request it from employer. File ITR with available documents and consult CA.
