## For Users

### How to Book an Appointment

#### Step 1: Browse Professionals
1. Log into your FinCart account
2. Go to **Dashboard** → **Professional Network** → **Find Professionals**
3. Browse available professionals by type:
   - **Chartered Accountant (CA)** - Tax advice, audits, compliance
   - **Tax Consultant** - Tax planning, ITR preparation, deductions
   - **Financial Advisor** - Investment planning, wealth management, budgeting

#### Step 2: View Professional Profile
1. Click **📅 Book Now** next to any professional
2. View their complete profile:
   - Name and type
   - Qualifications and experience
   - Location and languages
   - Biography and specializations
   - Consultation fee
3. Review their contact information

#### Step 3: Fill Booking Form
Click **Book Consultation** to open the booking form:

**Required Fields:**
- **Appointment Date** - Select a future date (minimum 1 day from today)
- **Appointment Time** - Select time between 9 AM and 6 PM
- **Consultation Type** - Choose:
  - Initial Consultation
  - Follow-up Consultation
  - Tax Planning
  - Investment Review
  - Other (add in notes)

**Optional Fields:**
- **Consultation Mode** - Select:
  - Online (Zoom/Google Meet/Phone)
  - In-Person (at professional's office)
  - Hybrid (call to arrange)
- **Additional Notes** - Add any specific information for the professional
  - Medical/health conditions, financial situation, specific concerns, etc.

#### Step 4: Confirm Booking
1. Review all appointment details
2. Check that:
   - Date is correct
   - Time works for you
   - Mode of consultation is acceptable
3. Click **Book Appointment**

#### Step 5: Confirmation Page
After successful booking:
- ✓ You'll see a confirmation page
- ✓ Check your email for confirmation
- ✓ The appointment shows as **⏳ Pending** (awaiting professional's acceptance)

### What Happens Next?

**Within 24 Hours:**
- Professional reviews your booking request
- They will either:
  - ✅ Accept → Status changes to "Confirmed"
  - ❌ Reject → Status changes to "Rejected" with reason

**If Accepted:**
- You'll receive a confirmation email
- Payment processing information will be sent
- Contact details and meeting link (if online) will be provided
- You can proceed with payment

**If Rejected:**
- You'll receive a notification with the reason
- You can book another appointment with the same or different professional
- Refund (if any payment made) will be processed

### View Your Appointments

Access your appointment history anytime:
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Professional Network** → **My Appointments**
2. See all your appointments organized by date
3. Each appointment card shows:
   - Professional name and type
   - Date and time
   - Appointment type and mode
   - Current status with icon:
     - ⏳ **Pending** - Awaiting professional's response
     - ✅ **Confirmed** - Professional accepted, ready for payment
     - ❌ **Rejected** - Professional declined (reason shown)
     - ⊘ **Cancelled** - You or professional cancelled

### Cancel an Appointment

**You can cancel if:**
- Status is **Pending** (not yet accepted)
- Status is **Confirmed** (already accepted)

**You cannot cancel if:**
- Status is **Rejected** or **Cancelled** (already complete)

**To Cancel:**
1. Go to **My Appointments**
2. Find the appointment to cancel
3. Click **Cancel** button
4. Confirm the cancellation
5. Professional will be notified

**Note:** If appointment was confirmed and paid, refund policy applies (check with professional).

### Rebook After Cancellation or Rejection

1. Go to **My Appointments**
2. Find the cancelled/rejected appointment
3. Click **Book Again** or **Rebook**
4. You'll be taken back to the professional's profile
5. Click **Book Consultation** again
6. Fill out new booking details
7. Submit the new appointment request

## For Professionals

### Access Your Dashboard

1. Log in with your professional account
2. Automatically redirected to **Professional Dashboard**
3. Your dashboard shows:
   - **Application Status** - Current approval status
   - **Your Profile** - Name, type, experience, contact info
   - **Appointment Requests** - All pending, confirmed, and rejected appointments

### View Appointment Requests

Your dashboard displays a table of all appointments:

**Table Columns:**
- **Client Name** - Full name and email address
- **Date & Time** - Formatted appointment date and time
- **Type** - Consultation type selected by client
- **Status** - Current status (Pending, Confirmed, Rejected, Cancelled)
- **Actions** - Buttons to manage the appointment

**Status Legend:**
- ⏳ **Pending** - Awaiting your response
- ✅ **Confirmed** - You accepted, client can proceed with payment
- ✗ **Rejected** - You declined (includes your reason)
- ⊘ **Cancelled** - Appointment was cancelled

### Accept an Appointment

When you receive a booking request:

1. **Review Details:**
   - Client name and contact
   - Requested appointment date and time
   - Consultation type and mode
   - Any notes the client provided

2. **Accept the Appointment:**
   - Click the green **Accept** button
   - The appointment status changes to ✅ **Confirmed**
   - Client receives a confirmation email with:
     - Your contact information
     - Payment instructions
     - Meeting link (if online)

3. **Follow Up:**
   - Check your email for backup notifications
   - Be ready to send meeting link/details to client

### Reject an Appointment

If you cannot accommodate the booking:

1. **Review Details:**
   - Check your calendar and availability
   - Check consultation type and mode
   - Read client's notes for any special requirements

2. **Reject the Appointment:**
   - Click the red **Reject** button
   - (Optional) Add a reason:
     - "Unavailable at that time"
     - "Consultation type not offered"
     - "Geographic area not served"
     - "Already fully booked"
     - Other custom reason

3. **Client Notification:**
   - Client receives rejection email with your reason
   - They can rebook with you or another professional
   - No payment required for rejected bookings

### Cancel a Confirmed Appointment

In urgent situations, you can cancel confirmed appointments:

1. Find the appointment in your dashboard
2. Click **Cancel** button
3. Client receives cancellation notification
4. Refund handled according to terms
5. Appointment marked as ⊘ **Cancelled**

**Best Practice:** Only cancel in emergencies (illness, emergency, etc.)

### Manage Your Availability

**Current System:** Manual appointment acceptance
- You review each booking request
- Accept only slots you can accommodate
- No pre-set availability

**Tips for Managing Bookings:**
- Check your email regularly for new bookings
- Review client notes for special requirements
- Respond within 24 hours
- Set clear expectations about consultation duration

## FAQs

### Booking Questions

**Q: How far in advance can I book an appointment?**
A: You can book appointments up to 6 months in advance. Minimum booking is 1 day in the future.

**Q: What if my preferred time slot is already booked?**
A: The system prevents double bookings. You'll see an error message and can select another time slot.

**Q: Can I book multiple appointments with the same professional?**
A: Yes, as long as the time slots don't conflict.

**Q: What if I don't know my preferred time yet?**
A: You can add a note asking the professional for available slots. Their response will help you book at a suitable time.

### Professional Response Questions

**Q: How long will it take for a professional to respond to my booking?**
A: Most professionals respond within 24 hours. Some may respond sooner. You'll receive an email notification when they respond.

**Q: What if the professional doesn't respond within 24 hours?**
A: You can cancel the booking and try another professional. Contact support if you need assistance.

**Q: Can I message the professional before booking?**
A: The current system doesn't have direct messaging. Use the Notes field to communicate initial questions. The professional can respond with a message in their confirmation/rejection email.

### Payment Questions

**Q: Do I pay before or after booking?**
A: Booking is free. Payment is processed after the professional confirms your appointment.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Payment details will be provided by the professional in their confirmation email. Check with them for available payment methods.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I cancel?**
A: Refund policy depends on when you cancel relative to the appointment date. Professional will explain terms in confirmation email.

### Cancellation Questions

**Q: When can I cancel an appointment?**
A: You can cancel anytime before or after the appointment (for refund eligibility).

**Q: Will I receive a refund?**
A: Refund eligibility depends on:
  - Status (Pending vs Confirmed)
  - Time until appointment
  - Professional's refund policy
  - Payment already made

Contact the professional directly for refund details.

**Q: Will the professional be notified if I cancel?**
A: Yes, the professional receives a cancellation notification immediately.

### Technical Questions

**Q: I didn't receive a confirmation email. What do I do?**
A: 
1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Check if you provided a correct email address
3. Wait 5-10 minutes for email to arrive
4. Go to your appointment history in the app to confirm booking was successful
5. Contact support if the appointment doesn't appear in the app

**Q: Can I change the appointment time after booking?**
A: No direct reschedule feature yet. Cancel the current booking and create a new one with the new date/time. The professional may manually accommodate this—mention it in notes.

**Q: Can I book on behalf of someone else?**
A: No, each account must book their own appointments. You cannot book for another person.

## Troubleshooting

### Booking Issues

**Problem: "Time slot already booked" error**
- **Cause:** Another user just booked the same slot
- **Solution:** Select a different time

**Problem: "Please select a future date" error**
- **Cause:** Selected date is in the past or today
- **Solution:** Select a date at least 1 day from today

**Problem: "Time must be between 9 AM and 6 PM" error**
- **Cause:** Selected time is outside business hours
- **Solution:** Choose a time between 9 AM and 6 PM

**Problem: Cannot see "Book Consultation" button**
- **Cause:** Professional account not logged in, or professional not active
- **Solution:** Log in as regular user, or check if professional is active

**Problem: Form submission hangs or times out**
- **Cause:** Server issue or poor internet connection
- **Solution:** 
  - Refresh the page
  - Check internet connection
  - Try again after 1-2 minutes
  - Contact support if issue persists

### Appointment History Issues

**Problem: Appointment not appearing in "My Appointments"**
- **Cause:** Booking failed silently, or page not refreshed
- **Solution:** 
  - Refresh the page (Ctrl+R)
  - Check your email for confirmation
  - Book appointment again if not found

**Problem: Cannot cancel appointment**
- **Cause:** Status is Rejected or already Cancelled
- **Solution:** You can only cancel Pending or Confirmed appointments

**Problem: Cancellation not working**
- **Cause:** Form submission error
- **Solution:** 
  - Refresh the page
  - Try cancellation again
  - Check your spam email for cancellation confirmation
  - Contact support if issue persists

### Email Issues

**Problem: Not receiving confirmation emails**
- **Cause:** 
  - Email address incorrect
  - Emails going to spam folder
  - Email server configuration
- **Solution:**
  - Check spam/junk folder
  - Verify email address in profile
  - Wait 5-10 minutes
  - Contact support with your email address

**Problem: Receiving duplicate emails**
- **Cause:** System sent email twice due to network issue
- **Solution:** Normal, can be ignored. This rarely happens.

**Problem: Email contains raw HTML code instead of formatted text**
- **Cause:** Email client configuration
- **Solution:** Email client should render HTML automatically. If not, contact email provider support.
